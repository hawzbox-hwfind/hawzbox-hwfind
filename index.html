Help & Support

How to Post a House?

Tap the + button in bottom center. Add State, Area, full address, House Type, Price, Listing Type (Rent/Sale). Select 3-5 high quality photos and optional video tour. Choose amenities: Constant Water, Steady Power, Security, etc. Add custom amenities and landlord details, then POST NOW. Your listing goes live instantly on Supabase.

How to Find a House?

Use search bar at top to search by area or house type. Use filter chips (All, Self Contain, Single Room etc) or tap filter icon to set price range, state, area, listing type. Tap any card to see full details, amenities, video tour, and landlord contact after verification.

Saving & My Listings

Tap bookmark icon on detail page to save a house to Saved Properties. Check Saved Properties from menu to view full details again anytime. My Listings shows only houses YOU posted. If a house is taken, tap Delete Post - it deletes from feed and Supabase to free space.

FAQs

Q: Is HawzBox free?
Yes, posting and searching is free. Contact reveal may require small verification.

Q: How to delete a post?
Go to My Listings, tap Delete Post badge on the house taken. It removes from everywhere.

Q: Are houses verified?
We encourage real photos and videos. Report fake listings via chat.

Privacy

HawzBox respects your privacy. We only collect house listing data and contact info you provide. Your phone and WhatsApp are only shown after paid verification.
